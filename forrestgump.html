Forrest Gump

Forrest Gump

Release Date: 6 July 1994

Director: Robert Zemeckis

Main Cast: Tom Hanks, Robin Wright, Gary Sinise

IMDb Rating: 8.8/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

The presidencies of Kennedy and Johnson, the Vietnam War, the Watergate scandal, and other historical events unfold from the perspective of an Alabama man with an extraordinary life and an IQ of 75.

My Advice: Watch it for the emotional journey, the incredible performance by Tom Hanks, and a story that perfectly mixes comedy, tragedy, and history. It's a beautiful reminder that life is full of surprises.

Interesting Facts

• Tom Hanks won the Academy Award for Best Actor for this role, making it his second consecutive win (after Philadelphia).

• The movie won 6 Oscars in total, including Best Picture and Best Director.

• The film is based on the 1986 novel by Winston Groom. However, the movie changes a lot of things, including making Forrest a much nicer and more innocent character.

• The famous line "Life is like a box of chocolates" is actually a misquote from the book, which originally said "Life is like a box of chocolates, you never know what you're gonna get."
