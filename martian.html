Release Date: 2 October 2015

Director: Ridley Scott

Main Cast: Matt Damon, Jessica Chastain, Kristen Wiig

IMDb Rating: 8.0/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

An astronaut is presumed dead and left behind on Mars. He must use his wits and scientific knowledge to survive and find a way to signal Earth that he's alive. It's an incredibly smart and inspiring survival story.

My Advice: Watch it for a smart, funny, and inspiring journey. It makes science look cool and celebrates human ingenuity and collaboration.

Interesting Facts

• The movie is based on Andy Weir's 2011 novel, which originally started as a free blog series before becoming a bestseller.

• NASA collaborated closely with the filmmakers to make the science and technology as accurate as possible.

• Matt Damon lost a significant amount of weight for the role, and his diet was strictly monitored by a nutritionist.

• The "potato farming" concept on Mars is scientifically plausible and was praised by real-life astronauts.
