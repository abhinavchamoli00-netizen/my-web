The Shawshank Redemption

The Shawshank Redemption

Release Date: 23 September 1994

Director: Frank Darabont

Main Cast: Tim Robbins, Morgan Freeman

IMDb Rating: 9.3/10 ⭐

Age Rating: R (18+)

Story & Advice

Two imprisoned men bond over a number of years, finding solace and eventual redemption through acts of common decency. This is a masterpiece of storytelling that explores hope, friendship, and the human spirit.

My Advice: Watch it if you believe in the power of hope. It's a film that will stay with you forever.

Interesting Facts

• The movie is based on Stephen King's novella "Rita Hayworth and Shawshank Redemption".

• Despite being widely considered the greatest film of all time, it was a box office flop when it released in 1994.

• Director Frank Darabont financed the opening scene (the car scene) out of his own pocket.

• In the original book, Red (Morgan Freeman's character) is an Irishman, but the role was specifically changed for Freeman.
