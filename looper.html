Looper

Looper

Release Date: 28 September 2012

Director: Rian Johnson

Main Cast: Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Bruce Willis, Emily Blunt

IMDb Rating: 7.4/10 ⭐

Age Rating: R (18+)

Story & Advice

In a future where time travel is invented, the mob sends its targets back in time to be killed by "loopers". Joe is a looper whose future self escapes, leading to a dangerous confrontation.

My Advice: Watch it for a smart, gritty time-travel movie that focuses on characters and consequences. The performances are top-notch and the story will keep you hooked.

Interesting Facts

• Joseph Gordon-Levitt wore prosthetics and practiced Bruce Willis's mannerisms to look and act like a young version of him.

• Director Rian Johnson wrote the script in just a few weeks, and it became one of the most talked-about sci-fi scripts in Hollywood.

• The time travel rules are intentionally kept vague by the director to focus more on the characters rather than the mechanics of time travel.

• The makeup for Joseph Gordon-Levitt to look like Bruce Willis took 3 hours to apply every single day.
