Iron Man

Iron Man

Release Date: 2 May 2008

Director: Jon Favreau

Main Cast: Robert Downey Jr., Gwyneth Paltrow, Jeff Bridges

IMDb Rating: 7.9/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

After being held captive in an Afghan cave, billionaire engineer Tony Stark creates a unique weaponized suit of armor to fight evil.

My Advice: Watch it to see where the entire Marvel Cinematic Universe began. Robert Downey Jr.'s performance is iconic.

Interesting Facts

• Robert Downey Jr. was not the first choice for Iron Man. The studio wanted Tom Cruise or Nicolas Cage.

• Most of the dialogue in the movie was improvised by the actors, as the script was not fully finished when filming began.

• The iconic "I am Iron Man" line at the end was completely improvised by RDJ.
