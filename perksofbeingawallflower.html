The Perks of Being a Wallflower

The Perks of Being a Wallflower

Release Date: 21 September 2012

Director: Stephen Chbosky

Main Cast: Logan Lerman, Emma Watson, Ezra Miller

IMDb Rating: 7.9/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

A shy freshman is taken under the wing of two seniors who welcome him to the real world. It's a touching and relatable coming-of-age story about friendship, love, and mental health.

My Advice: Watch it for the raw and honest portrayal of teenage life. The performances are incredible, and the film handles sensitive topics with a lot of heart and care.

Interesting Facts

• The film is based on the bestselling novel by Stephen Chbosky, who also wrote the screenplay and directed the film.

• Emma Watson took the role of Sam to break away from her "Harry Potter" image and prove her range as an actress.

• The famous "tunnel song" scene was shot using a real truck and a moving camera rig to capture the raw emotion of the moment.

• The movie deals with sensitive topics like mental health, abuse, and sexuality with a lot of authenticity and sensitivity.
