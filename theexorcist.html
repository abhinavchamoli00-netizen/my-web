The Exorcist

🚨
EXTREME CONTENT WARNING

This film contains intense demonic possession, graphic bodily harm, disturbing sexual and blasphemous content, extreme psychological horror, and imagery that has traumatized viewers for decades. Not suitable for minors, sensitive viewers, or anyone with religious or psychological sensitivities. Watch at your own risk. For time-pass only.

Release Date: 26 December 1973

Director: William Friedkin

Cast: Ellen Burstyn, Max von Sydow, Linda Blair, Jason Miller, Lee J. Cobb, Kitty Winn, Jack MacGowran

Genre: Horror / Supernatural / Psychological

Runtime: 122 minutes

IMDb: 8.1 / 10

Story: In Georgetown, Washington D.C., a 12-year-old girl named Regan MacNeil begins exhibiting bizarre and violent behaviour. Her actress mother Chris tries every medical and psychiatric avenue, but nothing explains the transformation. When modern science fails, she turns to two Catholic priests — Father Merrin, an experienced exorcist, and Father Karras, a young priest struggling with his own faith. What follows is one of the most terrifying battles between good and evil ever put on film.

Interesting Facts:

• Often called the scariest movie ever made. Won 2 Oscars and was nominated for 10, including Best Picture.

• Set crews reported strange occurrences during filming — fires, injuries, and deaths in related families. Many believe the production was cursed.

• Linda Blair was only 12 during filming. She wore full-body makeup and prosthetics for the possession scenes.

• The film was banned in several countries and even today remains restricted in some regions.

• The "spider walk" scene was cut from the original release and only restored in the 2000 director's cut.

• Ellen Burstyn suffered a permanent back injury during the scene where she is thrown across the room.
