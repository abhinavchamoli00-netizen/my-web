Fight Club

Fight Club

Release Date: 10 September 1999

Director: David Fincher

Main Cast: Brad Pitt, Edward Norton, Helena Bonham Carter

IMDb Rating: 8.8/10 ⭐

Age Rating: R (18+)

Story & Advice

An insomniac office worker and a devil-may-care soap maker form an underground fight club that evolves into much, much more. It's a dark, satirical take on consumer culture and modern masculinity.

My Advice: Watch it with an open mind. It's a film that redefined cult cinema and has one of the most shocking plot twists in movie history.

Interesting Facts

• The movie is based on Chuck Palahniuk's 1996 novel of the same name.

• Brad Pitt and Edward Norton actually took real boxing, taekwondo, and soap-making lessons for their roles.

• It was a box office disappointment when it first released, but became a massive cult classic on DVD.

• The author of the book, Chuck Palahniuk, openly admitted that the movie's ending was better than his own book's ending.
