Into the Wild

Release Date: 19 October 2007

Director: Sean Penn

Main Cast: Emile Hirsch, Marcia Gay Harden, William Hurt, Vince Vaughn

IMDb Rating: 8.1/10 ⭐

Age Rating: R (18+)

Story & Advice

After graduating from Emory University, top student and athlete Christopher McCandless abandons his possessions, gives his entire $24,000 savings account to charity and hitchhikes to Alaska to live in the wilderness.

My Advice: Watch it for a profound journey of self-discovery, freedom, and the harsh realities of nature. It will make you think about your own life and what truly matters.

Interesting Facts

• The film is based on the true story of Christopher McCandless, documented in Jon Krakauer's 1996 non-fiction book of the same name.

• Sean Penn spent nearly a decade trying to get the film made, waiting for the McCandless family's approval.

• Emile Hirsch lost 41 pounds (18 kg) for the role to accurately portray McCandless's physical transformation during his journey.

• The film was shot entirely on location, following the actual route McCandless took across America, Mexico, and up to Alaska.
