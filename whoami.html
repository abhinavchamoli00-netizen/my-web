Who Am I

Release Date: 25 September 2014

Director: Baran bo Odar

Main Cast: Tom Schilling, Elyas M'Barek, Hannah Herzsprung

IMDb Rating: 7.4/10 ⭐

Age Rating: 15+

Story & Advice

A young, gifted hacker is recruited by a group of fellow outcasts to pull off a series of increasingly audacious cyber-attacks, drawing the attention of both law enforcement and a dangerous underworld.

My Advice: Watch it for a stylish, fast-paced German techno-thriller with a cool, rebellious spirit. It's a fun ride with a few clever twists.

Khaasiyat / Interesting Facts

• The movie is a German production and became a massive cult hit internationally, especially among tech enthusiasts.

• The film accurately portrays real hacking techniques like social engineering, keylogging, and DDoS attacks, making it a favorite among cybersecurity fans.

• The mask used in the film is a Guy Fawkes mask, which is also famously associated with the real-life hacker collective "Anonymous".

• The film explores themes of modern cyber-anarchy, anti-capitalism, and the power of information in the digital age.
