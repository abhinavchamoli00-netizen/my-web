Thor

Thor

Release Date: 6 May 2011

Director: Kenneth Branagh

Main Cast: Chris Hemsworth, Tom Hiddleston, Natalie Portman

IMDb Rating: 7.0/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

The powerful but arrogant warrior Thor is cast out of the fantastic realm of Asgard by his father Odin and sent to live amongst humans on Earth, where he soon becomes one of their finest defenders.

My Advice: Watch it to see the incredible casting of Chris Hemsworth and Tom Hiddleston (Loki), who became fan favorites.

Interesting Facts

• Tom Hiddleston originally auditioned for the role of Thor, but director Kenneth Branagh asked him to audition for Loki instead.

• Chris Hemsworth gained over 20 pounds of muscle for the role of Thor.

• Kenneth Branagh is a renowned Shakespearean actor and director, which brought a theatrical, Shakespearean feel to the family drama of Asgard.
