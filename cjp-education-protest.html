📌 Executive Summary

Between June 6 and July 25, 2026, the youth-led satirical movement Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) held a sustained protest at Jantar Mantar, New Delhi, demanding accountability following the alleged NEET-UG 2026 paper leak. The movement gained national attention and ended with the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan on July 25, 2026.

This report compiles publicly reported events, statements, and outcomes. Where a claim is contested or unverified, it is marked as such. Please see the Confirmed vs Disputed section for a breakdown.

🔥 Background: The Trigger

The immediate trigger of the movement was the reported NEET-UG 2026 paper leak. Following widespread allegations of irregularities, the exam was cancelled in early May 2026.

Reports in the weeks following the cancellation documented multiple student deaths by suicide across India. The CJP publicly stated that 24 students died nationwide, including 6 in Uttar Pradesh alone. Figure reported by protest organisers — independent verification pending.

The CJP was formed in May 2026, in part as a satirical response to a reported courtroom remark comparing unemployed youth to "cockroaches." The remark's exact context is disputed. Verify: original court transcript.

🎯 Protest Demands

The CJP publicly listed these demands:

  1. Resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan.
  2. ₹1 crore compensation for each family of students who died by suicide.
  3. Criminal accountability for those responsible for the paper leak.

Status: Following the minister's resignation, CJP publicly stated that all three demands had been accepted by the government. Formal government confirmation of the compensation and prosecution components is pending independent verification.

🏛️ Outcome & Aftermath

The resignation of the Union Education Minister on July 25, 2026, was reported as the movement's principal outcome. CJP subsequently called off the protest.

In the weeks following, further developments were reported, including:

  • August 9, 2026 — Reported CJP support for a hunger strike in Jharkhand.
  • October 9, 2026 — A Supreme Court order regarding an independent probe into the July police action. Details and scope pending primary source.
  • October 10, 2026 — CJP's follow-up protest ("Jantar Mantar 2.0") demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. See full report.

Unresolved questions: whether the three demands have been formally implemented; status of FIRs against protesters; independent count of injuries and deaths.
