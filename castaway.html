Cast Away

Release Date: 22 December 2000

Director: Robert Zemeckis

Main Cast: Tom Hanks, Helen Hunt

IMDb Rating: 7.8/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

A FedEx executive undergoes a physical and emotional transformation after crash landing on a deserted island. It's a powerful story about survival, hope, and the indomitable human spirit.

My Advice: Watch it for Tom Hanks's incredible solo performance. It's a masterclass in acting and a deeply moving journey of self-discovery and resilience.

Khaasiyat / Interesting Facts

• Tom Hanks lost 50 pounds (22 kg) for the middle part of the film and then had to gain it all back. Production was halted for a full year just so he could lose the weight and grow his hair out.

• The volleyball "Wilson" was named after the sports equipment company, and it became one of the most iconic "characters" in movie history.

• Tom Hanks got a severe staph infection during filming from a cut on his leg while shooting the island scenes.

• The film was shot on a real remote island in Fiji, which added to the authenticity and the challenges faced by the cast and crew.
