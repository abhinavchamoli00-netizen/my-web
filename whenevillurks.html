When Evil Lurks

🚨
EXTREME CONTENT WARNING

This film contains extreme demonic horror, graphic and shocking violence, disturbing deaths including of children and animals, psychological terror, and relentless dread. Not suitable for minors, sensitive viewers, or anyone disturbed by possession themes. Watch at your own risk. For time-pass only.

Release Date: 27 October 2023

Director: Demián Rugna

Cast: Ezequiel Rodríguez, Demián Salomón, Silvina Sabater, Luis Ziembrowski, Paula Rubinsztein

Genre: Horror / Supernatural / Folk Horror

Runtime: 99 minutes

IMDb: 6.9 / 10

Story: In a remote rural village in Argentina, two brothers discover the body of a man horrifically deformed and infected with a demonic entity — a "Rotten," moments away from giving birth to evil itself. Desperate to stop the spread, they try to dispose of the body. But their attempt goes wrong, and the evil escapes, spreading like a plague. As panic grips the community, the brothers race to protect their families — only to learn that in this world, evil follows no rules and spares no one.

Interesting Facts:

• Widely praised as one of the best horror films of 2023, holding a 99% rating on Rotten Tomatoes.

• Director Demián Rugna previously made Terrified (2017), also considered a modern horror gem.

• The film establishes a unique mythology: demonic possession is a "contagion" with strict rules — and breaking any rule makes things dramatically worse.

• Several scenes were so shocking that festival audiences openly gasped and walked out.

• Shot on a limited budget in rural Argentina, yet praised for its atmosphere and practical effects.

• Stephen King publicly praised the film, calling it "brutal" and "genuinely terrifying."
