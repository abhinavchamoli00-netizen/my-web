Spider-Man 2

Spider-Man 2

Release Date: 30 June 2004

Director: Sam Raimi

Main Cast: Tobey Maguire, Alfred Molina, Kirsten Dunst

IMDb Rating: 7.5/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

Peter Parker is beset with troubles in his failing personal life as he battles a brilliant scientist named Doctor Otto Octavius, who becomes the villain Doctor Octopus after an experiment goes wrong.

My Advice: Watch it because many fans consider this the best Spider-Man movie ever made. Alfred Molina's Doc Ock is a fantastic villain.

Khaasiyat / Interesting Facts

• Alfred Molina's performance as Doc Ock is considered one of the best villain performances in superhero movie history.

• The famous train fight scene is still praised for its incredible practical effects and choreography.

• The movie was so well-received that it was nominated for an Academy Award for Best Visual Effects.
