Story & Advice

A thief who steals corporate secrets through the use of dream-sharing technology is given the inverse task of planting an idea into the mind of a C.E.O. It's a mind-bending heist film that plays with the concept of time and reality.

My Advice: Watch it for the incredible action sequences and the intellectual challenge. Don't blink, or you might miss a crucial detail!

Interesting Facts

• The film's famous ending with the spinning top is intentionally left ambiguous by Christopher Nolan, sparking endless debate among fans.

• The incredible "hallway fight" scene where the room rotates was filmed practically using a massive rotating set (a gimbal), not CGI.

• Christopher Nolan spent nearly 10 years writing and perfecting the script for this movie.

• The concept of time moving slower in deeper dreams is loosely based on real theories of how our brains perceive time while sleeping.
