Event Horizon

⚠️
Content Warning

This film contains intense horror imagery, graphic violence, disturbing psychological content, and brief sexual/nudity elements. Not recommended for sensitive viewers, minors, or anyone easily disturbed by body horror or psychological terror. Viewer discretion is strongly advised.

Release Date: 15 August 1997

Director: Paul W. S. Anderson

Cast: Laurence Fishburne, Sam Neill, Kathleen Quinlan, Joely Richardson, Richard T. Jones, Jack Noseworthy, Jason Isaacs, Sean Pertwee

Genre: Sci-Fi Horror / Psychological Horror

Runtime: 96 minutes

IMDb: 6.6 / 10

Story: It's the year 2047. A distress signal is picked up from the Event Horizon — a starship that vanished seven years ago on its maiden voyage. The rescue vessel Lewis and Clark is sent to investigate, carrying a crew that includes Dr. William Weir, the very man who designed the Event Horizon's experimental gravity drive. What they find is a ship that has returned from somewhere beyond our universe — a dimension of pure chaos. And something has come back with it.

Interesting Facts:

• The original cut reportedly ran 130 minutes. The studio forced it down to 96, removing the most disturbing footage.

• The film was inspired by The Shining, Alien, and Hellraiser.

• Sam Neill's iconic line — "Where we're going, we don't need eyes to see" — is one of the most quoted lines in sci-fi horror.

• A box-office disappointment at release, it later became a cult classic on home video.
