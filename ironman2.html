Iron Man 2

Iron Man 2

Release Date: 7 May 2010

Director: Jon Favreau

Main Cast: Robert Downey Jr., Mickey Rourke, Scarlett Johansson

IMDb Rating: 6.9/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

With the world now aware of his identity as Iron Man, Tony Stark must contend with both his declining health and a vengeful mad man with ties to his father's legacy.

My Advice: Watch it for the introduction of Black Widow (Scarlett Johansson) and the expansion of the MCU lore.

Interesting Facts

• This movie marked the MCU debut of Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff (Black Widow).

• Elon Musk made a cameo appearance in the movie, and parts of the film were actually shot at SpaceX headquarters.

• Mickey Rourke's character, Ivan Vanko, was a combination of two different Marvel villains: Whiplash and Crimson Dynamo.
