Meet Joe Black

Meet Joe Black

Release Date: 13 November 1998

Director: Martin Brest

Main Cast: Brad Pitt, Anthony Hopkins, Claire Forlani

IMDb Rating: 7.2/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

Death, who takes the form of a young man killed in an accident, asks a media mogul to act as his guide to teach him about life on Earth and, in the process, falls in love with the mogul's daughter.

My Advice: Watch it for the emotional depth, the beautiful cinematography, and the chemistry between Brad Pitt and Claire Forlani. It's a slow-burn, thought-provoking film about life and love.

Interesting Facts

• The film is a remake of the 1934 classic "Death Takes a Holiday".

• Brad Pitt reportedly disliked his own performance in the movie, particularly the accent he used for the character of Joe Black.

• The film's runtime is nearly 3 hours long, but director Martin Brest insisted on the length to fully develop the characters.

• The famous "peanut butter" scene is often cited as one of Brad Pitt's most awkward but iconic moments on screen.
