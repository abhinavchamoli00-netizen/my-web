The Prestige

Release Date: 20 October 2006

Director: Christopher Nolan

Main Cast: Hugh Jackman, Christian Bale, Michael Caine, Scarlett Johansson

IMDb Rating: 8.5/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

After a tragic accident, two stage magicians in 1890s London engage in a battle to create the ultimate illusion while sacrificing everything they have to outwit each other.

My Advice: Watch it twice. Once for the story, and once to appreciate all the clues you missed the first time. It's a brilliant puzzle.

Interesting Facts

• The film is based on the 1995 novel of the same name by Christopher Priest.

• Christopher Nolan's brother, Jonathan Nolan, co-wrote the screenplay with him.

• The legendary musician David Bowie played the role of Nikola Tesla. It was one of his last major film roles.

• The movie's structure itself is a magic trick, divided into three parts: The Pledge, The Turn, and The Prestige.
