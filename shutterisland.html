Shutter Island

Shutter Island

Release Date: 19 February 2010

Director: Martin Scorsese

Main Cast: Leonardo DiCaprio, Mark Ruffalo, Ben Kingsley

IMDb Rating: 8.2/10 ⭐

Age Rating: R (18+)

Story & Advice

Two U.S. Marshals travel to a remote island asylum for the criminally insane to investigate the disappearance of a patient. As they dig deeper, a complex and terrifying truth begins to unravel.

My Advice: Watch it for Leonardo DiCaprio's powerhouse performance and a story that will keep you guessing until the very last second. You will want to watch it twice!

Khaasiyat / Interesting Facts

• The movie is based on the 2003 novel of the same name by Dennis Lehane.

• This was the fourth collaboration between director Martin Scorsese and Leonardo DiCaprio.

• The film's ending is one of the most debated in cinema history, leaving audiences to argue whether it was a happy ending or a tragic one.

• Scorsese built a massive, fully functional lighthouse set for the film, which was one of the largest sets ever built for a movie.
