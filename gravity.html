Gravity

Release Date: 4 October 2013

Director: Alfonso Cuarón

Main Cast: Sandra Bullock, George Clooney

IMDb Rating: 7.7/10 ⭐

Age Rating: PG-13 (13+)

Story & Advice

A medical engineer and an astronaut work together to survive after an accident leaves them adrift in orbit. It's a visually stunning and intensely tense survival thriller.

My Advice: Watch it for the incredible cinematography and a gripping performance by Sandra Bullock. It's a masterclass in tension and visual storytelling.

Interesting Facts

• The film took over four years to make because of the complex technology required to simulate zero gravity and create realistic CGI.

• The script was originally written by Alfonso Cuarón's son, Jonás Cuarón, and was initially meant to be a much smaller project.

• Real-life astronaut Buzz Aldrin praised the film for its accuracy in depicting space travel, though he noted a few minor inaccuracies.

• The character of Dr. Ryan Stone was specifically written with Sandra Bullock in mind, though she almost turned down the role.
