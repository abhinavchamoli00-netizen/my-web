Story & Advice

A science teacher wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory of how he got there, only to discover he's on a mission to save the sun and all of humanity. It's a feel-good, optimistic space adventure.

My Advice: Watch it for a smart, funny, and genuinely heartwarming sci-fi story. The bond between the main character and an alien friend is the heart of the film.

Khaasiyat / Interesting Facts

• The movie is based on Andy Weir's bestselling novel of the same name, who also wrote "The Martian".

• The book is known for its heavy use of real science and problem-solving, making it a treat for sci-fi fans.

• Ryan Gosling is playing the lead role of Ryland Grace. The film is directed by the duo behind "Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse".

• The story features a unique alien character named "Rocky" and their friendship with the protagonist is the emotional core of the movie.
