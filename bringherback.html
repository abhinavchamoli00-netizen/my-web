Bring Her Back

🚨
EXTREME CONTENT WARNING

This film contains disturbing ritualistic horror, graphic violence and gore, child endangerment, intense psychological terror, and deeply unsettling imagery. Not suitable for minors, sensitive viewers, or anyone disturbed by cult or ritual themes. Watch at your own risk. For time-pass only.

Release Date: 30 May 2025

Directors: Danny Philippou, Michael Philippou

Cast: Billy Barratt, Sally Hawkins, Mischa Heywood

Genre: Horror / Mystery / Supernatural

Runtime: 104 minutes

IMDb: 7.1 / 10

Story: After the death of their father, a young man named Andy tries to gain custody of his half-sister Piper, who is partially blind. Placed temporarily in the foster care of Laura, a counsellor known for handling difficult cases, the two siblings soon discover something horrifying hidden inside her secluded home. What begins as a strange domestic arrangement spirals into a terrifying descent into ritualistic horror — one that forces the siblings to fight for their lives and their souls.

Interesting Facts:

• Directed by the Philippou twins, the same duo behind the hit horror film Talk to Me (2022).

• Sally Hawkins delivers a career-defining terrifying performance, far from her usual dramatic roles.

• The film premiered at the 2025 Cannes Film Festival and received instant buzz for its disturbing content.

• Practical effects were used extensively — very little CGI — creating a raw, unsettling realism.

• A24 produced the film, continuing their streak of boundary-pushing horror cinema.

• Multiple reviewers walked out of early screenings due to the intensity of certain scenes.
